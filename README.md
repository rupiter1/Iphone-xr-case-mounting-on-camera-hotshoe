# Iphone-xr-case-mounting-on-camera-hotshoe
im building a case to hold my iphone xr and fit it to my sony a6400 so i can use it as a monitor. im creating this great but slighty messy project because i love to make the best use out of the gear i have. and i dont have a montior right now so best use a phone!!
completed this project enough to ship a Version 1

first the idea popped into my mind when i heard that you could use your phone as a monitor for your camera, it clicked right away. i had to! so i hoped on onshape and grinded out a design. the first design was somewhat close but not there. too small, no room for the camera lens, i even had to cut alot off the end of the case because my printer really likes to warp my prints. second design, mostly the same, but a little better. third design is where it comes together. i added a thiner track for the lens to go into the case and a hole for it to sit in while sitting in the case. i made the case thinner over all and left extra room on the inside so i could put tape to hold the phone softly and stop the phone from falling out. and on the finial design (V4) i added holds in the cage for the power button and volume. making the phone useable even in the case. and i used a gopro style mount that i made, on both the camera side and the case side which means i can easily adjust the angle of the monitor. And i made the top thicker only adding materiel where it was needed.
